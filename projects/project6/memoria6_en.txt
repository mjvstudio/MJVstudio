The project is the full renovation of a family-run pizzeria in Encarnación, whose main problem was a poorly functional layout that hindered the workflow and the proper organization of tasks, in addition to lacking a restroom.

The intervention was conceived as a series of architectural micro-surgeries: targeted demolitions and new construction that made it possible to reorganize the space without losing the essence of the venue.

The proposal was developed by following the journey of the pizza from its preparation to its delivery to the customer, optimizing circulation, the relationship between work areas and operational efficiency.

Through a strategic redistribution, a more fluid, functional and orderly space was achieved, incorporating service and hygiene needs.

The renovation shows how precise interventions can transform the dynamics of an everyday business, improving the experience of both those who work there and its customers, without losing the warm, family character that defines it.

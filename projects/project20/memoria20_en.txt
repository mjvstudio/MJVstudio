<h2>Project Description:</h2>
<p>The Vázquez Automotores project, designed by MJV Arquitectura y Diseño, stands as an architectural landmark on Avenida Irrazábal in Encarnación. More than just a car dealership, the design combines modernity, elegance and sustainability, bringing dynamism to its surroundings and improving the user experience.

Its red façade and steel structure stand out in the urban landscape, becoming a visual focal point. The large glazed openings bring transparency and spaciousness, while the lighting highlights the architectural details both by day and by night. Outdoors, the vehicle display reinforces the connection between the space and its commercial purpose.

The central lapacho tree, located in the waiting area, creates a fresh and welcoming atmosphere, accompanied by strategically placed furniture. Its presence not only beautifies the space but also provides shade and comfort, enhancing the client's experience.

The interior combines materials such as wood, metal and exposed brick, creating a warm and sophisticated atmosphere. Natural light improves productivity and reduces energy consumption. The integration of vegetation and soft colors brings harmony, while the metal roof structure optimizes thermal efficiency and allows rainwater harvesting, reaffirming the commitment to sustainability.</p>

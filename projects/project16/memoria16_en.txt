The project proposes the transformation and expansion of an existing building, originally a radio station, adding a dental clinic on the ground floor while keeping the radio use on the upper floor.

The intervention seeks to bring both programs together under a common architectural identity, with independent entrances and spaces designed to meet the specific needs of each activity, prioritizing comfort, functionality and the experience of their users.

The proposal tackles the climatic challenge of the northwest façade with a perforated common-brick wall and timber sunshades that control solar gain, create plays of light and shadow and give character to the whole. The addition of gardens and a terrace linked to the waiting area strengthens the connection with nature, bringing freshness and environmental quality.

In this way, the renovation gives new meaning to the existing structure through a contemporary architecture that combines identity, sustainability and well-being, turning the constraints of the site into design opportunities.

The project proposes a retreat home integrated into the natural setting of María Auxiliadora, Itapúa, where the architecture prioritizes the connection with the landscape, natural light and the continuity between interior and exterior spaces.

Its horizontal volume, defined by a pitched roof with generous eaves, combines with a timber structure, exposed brick enclosures and large glazed surfaces that visually bring the surroundings into everyday life.

The layout organizes the social areas in one integrated space, while the private rooms are arranged along the plan, favoring privacy and comfort. Timber lattices regulate the opening to the outside, providing sun protection and warmth.

The result is a serene and functional architecture, conceived to inhabit the landscape with simplicity, making use of noble materials and the existing vegetation as essential elements of the spatial experience.

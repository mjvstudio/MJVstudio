Developed in collaboration with @roca_aisa, this architectural proposal grows out of a family's wish to connect with nature through a home that combines Mediterranean warmth with a contemporary language.

The façade stands out for a large central arch that frames the views and defines the identity of the whole, accompanied by terracotta-toned walls, timber details and the leading presence of a lapacho tree that converses with the architecture. The arches extend to the rear area, creating continuity and unity in the design.

The layout makes the most of the lot to organize the social, private and service areas, incorporating interior gardens, semi-covered spaces and terraces that favor cross ventilation and the relationship between inside and outside.

Large windows increase the intake of natural light, while the light roof of the garage blends fluidly into the whole. The result is a warm, serene and functional home, where Mediterranean tradition meets the Paraguayan context to create spaces designed for well-being and the enjoyment of everyday life.

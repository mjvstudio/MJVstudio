<h2>Our Team</h2>
<p>At MJV, we believe architecture is built through collaboration. Our team brings together different skills, experiences and ways of seeing spaces, while sharing a common approach to each project: with sensitivity, sound judgment and care for the people who will inhabit it.</p>
<p>We work closely together, with complementary roles that bring architecture, interior design, management and project development into a shared vision, ensuring every decision has a clear purpose.</p>

<div class="team-member">
    <h3>Martín Villalba</h3>
    <h4>Creative Director · Architect</h4>
    <p>Martín leads MJV’s creative and architectural direction. His work focuses on transforming ideas into spaces, developing projects from their initial conception through to the decisions that bring them to life.</p>
    <p>His experience in design and construction allows him to maintain a comprehensive view of the process, understanding that a strong architectural proposal must strike a balance between concept, functionality, materiality and what can realistically be built.</p>
    <p>Within the studio, he plays an active role in defining each project’s identity and in the decisions that shape our approach to architecture.</p>
</div>

<div class="team-member">
    <h3>María José Vera</h3>
    <h4>Director of Organization and Management · Architect</h4>
    <p>Majo leads the organization and management of both the projects and the studio, bringing together people, processes and decisions so that every project can develop in an orderly and coherent way.</p>
    <p>Her work focuses particularly on planning, monitoring progress, coordination and communication, connecting the different areas involved in the architectural process. She also helps shape MJV’s identity and communication, ensuring that the way we work is as coherent as the architecture we propose.</p>
    <p>Her perspective complements the studio’s creative dimension with a strategic, people-centered approach to management. She recognizes that behind every project are people, needs, schedules and decisions that also require careful attention and support.</p>
</div>

<div class="team-member">
    <h3>Gonzalo Sugastti</h3>
    <h4>Architectural Designer · Architect</h4>
    <p>Gonzalo is one of MJV’s principal architectural designers and works closely with the team on the architectural development of its projects.</p>
    <p>He takes part in the different stages of the design process, from exploring ideas and concepts to developing and resolving proposals. His work combines architectural thinking, spatial judgment and attention to the specific needs of each project.</p>
    <p>His contribution brings another design perspective to the team, enriching design decisions and allowing projects to be approached from different viewpoints. At MJV, dialogue between designers is a fundamental part of the creative process: discussing, questioning, developing and rethinking an idea until we find the solution that best responds to each situation.</p>
</div>

<div class="team-member">
    <h3>María Benítez</h3>
    <h4>Interior and Architectural Designer · Architect</h4>
    <p>María brings architecture and interior design together within the design process, approaching spaces with sensitivity and an eye for detail.</p>
    <p>Her involvement spans both architectural design development and decisions about interior layouts, materials, proportions, furniture and the elements that shape the everyday experience of a space.</p>
    <p>Her perspective allows architecture and interior design to be understood as parts of a single experience rather than separate disciplines. In every project, she seeks to connect decisions about form and materials with the way people will live in, move through and make the space their own.</p>
</div>

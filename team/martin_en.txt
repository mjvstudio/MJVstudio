Martín Villalba

Creative Director · Architect

Martín leads MJV’s creative and architectural direction. His work focuses on transforming ideas into spaces, developing projects from their initial conception through to the decisions that bring them to life.

His experience in design and construction allows him to maintain a comprehensive view of the process, understanding that a strong architectural proposal must strike a balance between concept, functionality, materiality and what can realistically be built.

Within the studio, he plays an active role in defining each project’s identity and in the decisions that shape our approach to architecture.

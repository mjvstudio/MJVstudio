María José Vera

Director of Organization and Management · Architect

Majo leads the organization and management of both the projects and the studio, bringing together people, processes and decisions so that every project can develop in an orderly and coherent way.

Her work focuses particularly on planning, monitoring progress, coordination and communication, connecting the different areas involved in the architectural process. She also helps shape MJV’s identity and communication, ensuring that the way we work is as coherent as the architecture we propose.

Her perspective complements the studio’s creative dimension with a strategic, people-centered approach to management. She recognizes that behind every project are people, needs, schedules and decisions that also require careful attention and support.

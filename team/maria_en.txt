María Benítez

Interior and Architectural Designer · Architect

María brings architecture and interior design together within the design process, approaching spaces with sensitivity and an eye for detail.

Her involvement spans both architectural design development and decisions about interior layouts, materials, proportions, furniture and the elements that shape the everyday experience of a space.

Her perspective allows architecture and interior design to be understood as parts of a single experience rather than separate disciplines. In every project, she seeks to connect decisions about form and materials with the way people will live in, move through and make the space their own.

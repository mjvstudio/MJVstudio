Gonzalo Sugastti

Architectural Designer · Architect

Gonzalo is one of MJV’s principal architectural designers and works closely with the team on the architectural development of its projects.

He takes part in the different stages of the design process, from exploring ideas and concepts to developing and resolving proposals. His work combines architectural thinking, spatial judgment and attention to the specific needs of each project.

His contribution brings another design perspective to the team, enriching design decisions and allowing projects to be approached from different viewpoints. At MJV, dialogue between designers is a fundamental part of the creative process: discussing, questioning, developing and rethinking an idea until we find the solution that best responds to each situation.
